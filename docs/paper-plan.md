## Working title
Page-Level Handwriting Forgery Detection with Multi-Reference Verification and Calibrated Verdicts

## Contributions as the paper may claim them (hedged: "to our knowledge")
1. Page-level verifier: attention-pooled bag of ink patches, one or several reference pages, four fusion rules compared.
2. Content-matched hard negatives on CVL (all writers copy the same texts) plus kNN writer mining.
3. Calibrated three-way verdict (consistent / inconclusive / likely forged) with thresholds fixed on validation, likelihood-ratio style score.
4. Compact from-scratch ResNet-18 student trained with distillation from a frozen DINOv2-small teacher (teacher only in training).
5. Honest protocol: writer-disjoint splits, a never-tuned cross-dataset test (Firemaker), explicit limits.

## Not claimable (checked by web search 2026-11-05)
- Distillation itself (precedent in writer verification and signature verification, arXiv 2308.01136).
- Score-level fusion and calibrated LR with an inconclusive outcome (forensic precedent, e.g. CEDAR-FOX, Chu NIJ).
- Detection of AI-imitated forgeries of a specific writer (not evaluated; generator step dropped).
