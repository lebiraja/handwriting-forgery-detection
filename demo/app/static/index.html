Read this before you quote a result

  • It scores style similarity, not forgery directly. The model learned from pages written by other people. It has not been trained or tested on skilled imitations of one specific person.
  • "Likely forged" means "very unlike this writer". The thresholds were fitted on validation pages: accept above the 99th percentile of different-writer scores, reject below the 1st percentile of same-writer scores. Everything between is inconclusive.
  • Samples are validation pages from IAM and CVL, which also set the thresholds, so they look better than unseen data would. The held-out test and Firemaker results are reported separately in the paper.
  • Phone photos and other paper or pens differ from the training scans. Use flat, well lit, full-page images with only handwriting in view (no printed form text).
